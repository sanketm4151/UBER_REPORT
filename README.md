# 🚖 Uber Ride Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Uber Ride Analytics Dashboard** is a data analytics project developed using Microsoft Power BI to analyze ride bookings, customer behavior, driver performance, revenue, and cancellation patterns.

The dashboard transforms ride-booking data into meaningful business insights through interactive visualizations, key performance indicators (KPIs), and category-wise comparisons.

The primary objective is to understand booking trends, identify cancellation patterns, evaluate customer and driver ratings, and analyze revenue across different vehicle types and payment methods.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Analyze overall ride-booking performance.
* Evaluate booking status and cancellation trends.
* Identify customer and driver cancellation patterns.
* Analyze revenue and booking value.
* Compare customer and driver ratings across vehicle types.
* Understand payment method preferences.
* Evaluate vehicle-wise booking performance.
* Support data-driven operational decisions through interactive dashboards.

## 📊 Dashboard Overview

The Power BI report contains multiple pages dedicated to different aspects of ride-booking analytics.

### 1. 📍 Booking Analysis

Analyzes booking status and overall booking patterns.

**Key Analysis:**

* Booking status distribution
* Successful and cancelled bookings
* Booking trends
* Customer-related booking information
* Date-based filtering

**Business Objective:** Understand booking outcomes and identify patterns in successful and unsuccessful rides.

### 2. ❌ Cancellation Analysis

Examines ride cancellations by customers and drivers.

**Key Analysis:**

* Customer cancellation counts
* Driver cancellation counts
* Reasons for customer cancellations
* Reasons for driver cancellations
* Cancellation patterns over the selected period

**Business Objective:** Identify common cancellation reasons and potential opportunities to improve booking completion rates.

### 3. ⭐ Ratings Analysis

Evaluates customer and driver ratings across different vehicle types.

**Key Analysis:**

* Customer ratings by vehicle type
* Driver ratings by vehicle type
* Comparative rating trends

**Business Objective:** Understand service quality and identify vehicle categories that may require further investigation.

### 4. 💳 Payment Analysis

Analyzes payment methods and their relationship with booking performance.

**Key Analysis:**

* Payment method distribution
* Booking value by payment method
* Payment-related booking metrics

**Business Objective:** Understand customer payment preferences and evaluate booking value across payment categories.

### 5. 🚘 Vehicle Analysis

Examines vehicle categories, cancellation metrics, and booking value.

**Key Analysis:**

* Vehicle-wise booking performance
* Cancellation metrics by vehicle type
* Booking value by vehicle category

**Business Objective:** Compare vehicle categories and identify opportunities to improve operational performance.

### 6. 💰 Revenue Analysis

Focuses on booking value and revenue-related performance across vehicle categories.

**Key Analysis:**

* Booking value by vehicle type
* Vehicle-wise revenue contribution
* Comparative booking value analysis

**Business Objective:** Understand which vehicle categories contribute most to the overall booking value.
